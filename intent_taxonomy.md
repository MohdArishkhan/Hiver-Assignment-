# SpotifyCares Intent Taxonomy

## Purpose

This taxonomy defines the customer-support intents used by the
SpotifyCares support agent.

The taxonomy is derived from inspection of SpotifyCares customer
conversations in the Customer Support on Twitter dataset.

Target number of intents: 12.

---

## 1. PLAYBACK_ISSUE

Customer is unable to play, pause, skip, or continuously stream music.

Examples:
- Songs won't play
- Playback stops unexpectedly
- Songs skip automatically
- Pause/play controls don't work
- Music stops when another app is opened

---

## 2. APP_TECHNICAL_ISSUE

Problems with the Spotify application, web player, search,
crashes, errors, updates, or device-specific behavior.

Examples:
- App crashes
- Search produces an error
- Web player doesn't work
- Spotify won't launch
- Problems after an app update

---

## 3. ACCOUNT_LOGIN_SECURITY

Problems involving login, password reset, account access,
hacked accounts, usernames, or suspicious account activity.

Examples:
- Can't log in
- Password reset doesn't work
- Account was hacked
- Username is incorrect
- Account was logged out

---

## 4. PREMIUM_SUBSCRIPTION

Questions or problems related to Premium subscription status,
activation, cancellation, eligibility, or subscription access.

Examples:
- Premium isn't activated
- How to cancel Premium
- Premium expired
- Can't activate Premium
- Subscription status is incorrect

---

## 5. PAYMENT_BILLING

Problems involving charges, refunds, payment methods, pricing,
duplicate charges, or incorrect billing.

Examples:
- Charged multiple times
- Charged after cancellation
- Wrong Premium price
- PayPal doesn't work
- Credit/debit card problems

---

## 6. FAMILY_STUDENT_PLANS

Questions and problems specifically involving Family or Student plans.

Examples:
- Family invitation not received
- Family address verification
- Student discount
- Student charged the wrong price
- Family Premium access missing

---

## 7. MUSIC_CONTENT_AVAILABILITY

Problems involving missing songs, albums, artists, releases,
regional availability, or incorrect artist metadata.

Examples:
- Song isn't available
- Album disappeared
- Artist page is incorrect
- Music isn't available in a country
- Wrong songs under an artist

---

## 8. PLAYLIST_LIBRARY

Problems involving playlists, saved music, library organization,
playlist imports, or deleted playlists.

Examples:
- Playlist disappeared
- Playlist import failed
- Can't create a playlist
- Playlist songs missing
- Playlist organization problems

---

## 9. DOWNLOAD_OFFLINE

Problems involving downloading music or listening offline.

Examples:
- Downloads don't work
- Download limit reached
- SD card storage problems
- Offline music unavailable
- Download location problems

---

## 10. ADS_FREE_TIER

Problems or questions about advertisements and Free-tier listening.

Examples:
- Ads appearing for Premium users
- Too many advertisements
- Ad-free listening duration
- Free-tier limitations

---

## 11. FEATURE_REQUEST

Customer is requesting a new feature or suggesting a product change.

Examples:
- Add lyrics search
- Add artist blocking
- Add social features
- Add driving mode
- Add new playlist functionality

---

## 12. GENERAL_OTHER

Messages that don't clearly belong to another intent.

Examples:
- General feedback
- Thanks
- Very short/vague messages
- Miscellaneous questions
